### Licenses & Roles

1. Review the [Permission Sets documentation](https://docs.getdbt.com/docs/cloud/manage-access/enterprise-permissions#permission-sets)
2. Determine 1-2 permission levels you would like to grant
3. Navigate to `Account settings`→`Groups & licenses`
4. Create a new group, name it and assign the permission sets you wish
5. Save it
6. Navigate to `Account settings`→`Users`
7. Invite users to your account by adding in their email addresses
8. Add them to this new group before sending the invitations
